## 5.2

:::{.question}

> Twelve people are given two identical speakers, which they are asked
> to listen to for differences, if any. Suppose that these people
> answer simply by guessing. Find the probability that three people
> claim to have heard a difference between the two speakers.

:::

$$
\begin{align*}

n &= 12, \quad p = \tfrac12, \quad x = 3 &&
  \htmlClass{hljs-comment}{\textit{guessing implies prob. $\frac12$}} \\
\\

\mathbb{P}(X = 3) &= b(3; 12, \tfrac12) = \binom{12}{3} \left(\tfrac12\right)^3 \left(\tfrac12\right)^{12-3} &&
  \htmlClass{hljs-comment}{\textit{\{binomial distribution, Walpole \S5.2\}}} \\
                  &= \binom{12}{3} \left(\tfrac12\right)^{12} \\
                  &= (220) \left(\tfrac{1}{4096}\right) \\
                  &= \frac{220}{4096} = \frac{55}{1024} \\
\mathbb{P}(X = 3) &\approx 0.0537 \space_\blacksquare \\

\end{align*}
$$

## 5.5

:::{.question}

> According to _Chemical Engineering Progress_ (November 1990),
> approximately 30% of all pipework failures in chemical plants are
> caused by operator error. \
> \
> (a) What is the probability that out of the next 20 pipework
> failures at least 10 are due to operator error? \
> (b) What is the probability that no more than 4 out of 20 such
> failures are due to operator error? \
> (c) Suppose, for a particular plant, that out of the random sample of
> 20 such failures, exactly 5 are due to operator error. Do you feel
> that the 30% figure stated above applies to this plant? Comment.

:::

$$
\begin{align*}

p &\coloneqq 0.3 &&
  \htmlClass{hljs-comment}{\textit{prob. of operator errorcause }} \\
q &\coloneqq 0.7 &&
  \htmlClass{hljs-comment}{\textit{prob. of other cause}} \\
X &\coloneqq \{0,1,2,...,n\} &&
  \htmlClass{hljs-comment}{\textit{r.v. of operator error in $n$ failures}} \\
\\
\mathbb{P}(X \geq 10) &= 1 - \mathbb{P}(X < 10) &&
  \htmlClass{hljs-comment}{\textit{(a) at least 10 out of 20 are op. err.}} \\
                      &\space &&
  \htmlClass{hljs-comment}{\textit{10 or more is complement of less than 10}} \\
                      &= 1 - B(10; 20, 0.3) \\
                      &= 1 - \sum_{x=0}^9 b(x; 20, 0.3) &&
  \htmlClass{hljs-comment}{\textit{\{Walpole \S 5.2\} binomial sum}} \\
                      &= 1 - \sum_{x=0}^9 \begin{pmatrix} 20 \\ x \end{pmatrix} 0.3^x 0.7^{20 - x} \\
                      &= 1 - \begin{pmatrix} 20 \\ 0 \end{pmatrix} 0.3^0 0.7^{20 - 0} + \begin{pmatrix} 20 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix} 0.3^1 0.7^{20 - 1} \\
                      &+ \begin{pmatrix} 20 \\ 2 \end{pmatrix} 0.3^2 0.7^{20 - 2} + \begin{pmatrix} 20 \\ 3 \end{pmatrix} 0.3^3 0.7^{20 - 3} \\
                      &+ \begin{pmatrix} 20 \\ 4 \end{pmatrix} 0.3^4 0.7^{20 - 4} + \begin{pmatrix} 20 \\ 5 \end{pmatrix} 0.3^5 0.7^{20 - 5} \\
                      &+ \begin{pmatrix} 20 \\ 6 \end{pmatrix} 0.3^6 0.7^{20 - 6} + \begin{pmatrix} 20 \\ 7 \end{pmatrix} 0.3^7 0.7^{20 - 7} \\
                      &+ \begin{pmatrix} 20 \\ 8 \end{pmatrix} 0.3^8 0.7^{20 - 8} + \begin{pmatrix} 20 \\ 9 \end{pmatrix} 0.3^9 0.7^{20 - 9} \\
                      &\approx 1 - 0.9520 \\
\mathbb{P}(X \geq 10) &\approx 0.0480 \space_\blacksquare \\

\end{align*}
$$

parts b & c on next page...

$$
\begin{align*}

\mathbb{P}(X \leq 4) &= B(4; 20, 0.3) &&
  \htmlClass{hljs-comment}{\textit{(b) no more than 4 out of 20 are op. err.}} \\
                     &= \sum_{x=0}^4 b(x; 20, 0.3) \\
                     &= \sum_{x=0}^4 \begin{pmatrix} 20 \\ 4 \end{pmatrix} 0.3^x 0.7^{20 - x} \\
                     &= \begin{pmatrix} 20 \\ 4 \end{pmatrix} 0.3^0 0.7^{20 - 0} + \begin{pmatrix} 20 \\ 4 \end{pmatrix} 0.3^1 0.7^{20 - 1} \\
                     &+ \begin{pmatrix} 20 \\ 4 \end{pmatrix} 0.3^2 0.7^{20 - 2} + \begin{pmatrix} 20 \\ 4 \end{pmatrix} 0.3^3 0.7^{20 - 3} \\
                     &+ \begin{pmatrix} 20 \\ 4 \end{pmatrix} 0.3^4 0.7^{20 - 4} \\
\mathbb{P}(X \leq 4) &\approx 0.2375 \space_\blacksquare \\
\\
\frac{5}{20} &= 0.25 \approx 0.3 \space_\blacksquare &&
  \htmlClass{hljs-comment}{\textit{yes the 30\% rate applies, because}} \\
             &\space &&
  \htmlClass{hljs-comment}{\textit{5 out of 20 is relatively similar to 30\%}}

\end{align*}
$$
